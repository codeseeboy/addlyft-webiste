✦ OUR MISSION
We Believe Every Small Store Deserves Enterprise-Level Marketing
"Marketing that works for the store owner — not just the agency."
Small retail is the backbone of every neighborhood. Convenience stores, restaurants, liquor stores, smoke shops — these aren't just businesses, they're community hubs.
But they've been left behind by marketing technology built for Fortune 500 companies. Addlyft changes that. We built an AI-powered platform that gives any store owner the ability to create professional audio ads, video content, and social media campaigns — in minutes.
And then we built Addlyft Reach — so advertisers pay to play inside these stores, sending passive revenue back to the store owners. Everyone wins.
